# Automations — AI Revenue Agent

Workflow automation guidance for triggers, sequences, and reporting.

---

## Automation Components

### Lead Capture Triggers
- New lead entry from website form
- New inbound lead from email or LinkedIn
- Imported lead list
- CRM lead creation

### Qualification Logic
- Score by industry, revenue, employee count, geography
- Score by engagement: website visits, email opens, replies, calendar bookings
- Tag and route only qualified prospects

### Outreach Sequences
- Email sequence start trigger
- LinkedIn connection trigger
- SMS follow-up trigger
- Pause when meeting booked

### Booking Flow
- Send calendar availability when qualification threshold met
- Confirm meeting via email and calendar invite
- Send reminders before the call

### Reporting Workflows
- Weekly performance snapshot
- Lead volume and conversion reports
- Pipeline value updates
- Client status updates
