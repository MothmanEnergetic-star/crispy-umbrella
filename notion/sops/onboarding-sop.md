# Onboarding SOP — AI Revenue Agent

Standard operating procedure for client onboarding.

---

## Steps

1. Collect client intake data.
2. Define ICP and target personas.
3. Connect CRM and calendar systems.
4. Build lead enrichment rules.
5. Configure outreach templates.
6. Validate lead sources and data accuracy.
7. Run internal QA before go-live.

---

## Deliverables
- Client intake form completed.
- CRM mapping documented.
- Outreach templates approved.
- Go-live checklist signed off.
