# QA SOP — AI Revenue Agent

Standard operating procedure for quality assurance and launch validation.

---

## QA Checklist

### Lead Flow
- New leads ingest correctly.
- Enrichment data populates.
- Qualification scores apply.

### Outreach
- Email templates render correctly.
- LinkedIn/ SMS sequences trigger.
- Follow-up timing is correct.

### Booking
- Calendar availability is accurate.
- Meeting invites send.
- Confirmation email is received.

### CRM Sync
- Records map to the right fields.
- Status updates sync properly.
- Opportunity stages update.

### Reporting
- Metrics display correctly.
- Alerts trigger for failures.
