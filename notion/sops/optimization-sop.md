# Optimization SOP — AI Revenue Agent

Standard operating procedure for ongoing optimization and improvement.

---

## Optimization Cycle

1. Review performance metrics weekly.
2. Identify low-performing sequences.
3. Update outreach templates and timing.
4. Test new messaging and channels.
5. Adjust qualification thresholds.
6. Review closed-loop feedback from sales.

---

## Goals
- Improve response and engagement rates.
- Increase meetings booked.
- Raise pipeline quality.
- Reduce wasted outreach.
