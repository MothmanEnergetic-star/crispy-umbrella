# Reporting — AI Revenue Agent

Performance reporting and KPI guidance for the AI Revenue Agent.

---

## Core Metrics

### Lead Metrics
- Leads processed
- Leads qualified
- Leads engaged

### Meeting Metrics
- Meetings booked
- Meetings confirmed
- Show rate

### Revenue Metrics
- Pipeline value created
- Estimated deal value
- Conversion rate

### Automation Health
- Sequence completion rate
- Response rate
- Follow-up performance

---

## Reporting Cadence
- Daily: Lead intake and engagement overview
- Weekly: Qualified meetings, pipeline creation, key trends
- Monthly: ROI, campaign performance, optimization opportunities

---

## Report Types
- Client performance dashboard
- Automation health report
- Campaign results summary
- Executive snapshot
