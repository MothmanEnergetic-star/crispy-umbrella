# Client Onboarding — AI Revenue Agent

Guided onboarding process from client signup to go-live.

---

## Onboarding Steps

### 1. Client Intake
- Company name
- Contact name
- Email
- Phone
- Current CRM
- Current lead sources
- Monthly meeting target
- Key revenue goals

### 2. ICP Definition
- Ideal industries
- Revenue range
- Employee count
- Geography
- Role / title targets
- Pain points

### 3. System Setup
- CRM connection (HubSpot or GoHighLevel)
- Email integration
- Calendly setup
- Data enrichment tools
- Tracking and reporting setup

### 4. Outreach Framework
- Email templates
- LinkedIn scripts
- SMS messages
- Follow-up cadence

### 5. Go-Live Checklist
- Lead source validation
- Sequence readiness
- Calendar availability confirmed
- CRM field mapping verified
- Reporting dashboard set up
