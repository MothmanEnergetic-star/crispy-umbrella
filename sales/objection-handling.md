# Objection Handling — AI Revenue Agent

Complete objection handler library for sales calls.

---

## Objection 1: "We're not looking right now"

### Why They're Saying It
- They haven't thought about this problem.
- They're not in active buying mode.
- They're busy and dismissing.

### Your Response

**YOU:**
"I totally get it — most people aren't actively looking until they hit a specific pain point.

Quick question though: If you *were* to add 20–30 qualified sales calls per month without hiring anyone, would that be interesting?

That's what we do for similar companies. Even if you're not ready now, it might be worth a 15-minute coffee chat so you know what's possible. Deal?"

### Strategy
- Reframe as an option, not a need.
- Plant the seed.
- Ask for a low-commitment next step.

---

## Objection 2: "Your pricing is too high"

### Why They're Saying It
- They don't see the value yet.
- They're price-shopping.
- They're comparing to hiring an SDR.

### Your Response

**YOU:**
"I hear that — and fair question. Let me ask you this:

What's the cost of *not* having faster lead response? How many leads do you lose right now because you aren't responding in time?

If you're losing even 2–3 qualified meetings per month, the AI pays for itself in about 60 days.

Plus, most of our clients see ROI in the first 30 days."

### Strategy
- Connect pricing to ROI.
- Show the cost of the status quo.
- Focus on payback period.

---

## Objection 3: "We already have a tool for this"

### Why They're Saying It
- They use HubSpot, Pipedrive, or another CRM.
- They don't understand the difference.
- They see this as redundant.

### Your Response

**YOU:**
"I totally get it — and here's the thing: those tools are great for *managing* your pipeline. What we do is *fill* your pipeline.

This is the automation layer that creates qualified opportunities and books meetings while your existing systems keep deals moving."
