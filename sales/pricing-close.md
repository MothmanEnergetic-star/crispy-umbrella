# Pricing Close — AI Revenue Agent

Pricing close language and deal framing for sales conversations.

---

## Pricing Close Structure

### 1. Recap the value
**YOU:** "So what we're solving for is faster response, better qualification, and booked meetings without adding SDR headcount."

### 2. Anchor the investment
**YOU:** "Most clients start with a setup fee of $2,000–$10,000 depending on the complexity, and monthly pricing from $500–$5,000."

### 3. Frame the ROI
**YOU:** "If this saves you 20–30 qualified meetings per month, and your average deal size is $10k, the system pays for itself in the first 30–60 days."

### 4. Make the next step easy
**YOU:** "The next step is a quick pilot plan. We can scope the setup and agree on the first 60 days together. Does that sound reasonable?"

---

## Pricing Tier Messaging

### Startup
- Setup: $2,000
- Monthly: $500
- Best when you want a fast, simple launch with email outreach and CRM sync.

### Growth
- Setup: $5,000
- Monthly: $1,500
- Best when you want multi-channel outreach, advanced scoring, and reporting.

### Enterprise
- Setup: $10,000+
- Monthly: $3,000–$5,000
- Best for high-volume workflows, deep integrations, and dedicated support.
