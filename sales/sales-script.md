# Sales Script — AI Revenue Agent

Complete sales conversation script for calls, demos, and closing.

---

## Call Structure

1. **Opening** (1 min)
2. **Discovery Questions** (3–4 min)
3. **Transition** (30 sec)
4. **Demo Narrative** (5–7 min)
5. **Value Pitch** (2 min)
6. **Pricing Close** (2 min)
7. **Final Close** (1 min)

**Total:** 15–18 minutes

---

## Opening (1 min)

**YOU:**
"Thanks for taking the time today. I know you're busy, so I'll keep this focused.

I work with B2B SaaS companies and agencies that want to scale their sales without hiring more SDRs. What we're finding is that companies are replacing 80% of SDR tasks with an AI Revenue Agent that books qualified meetings automatically.

In the next 15 minutes, I want to show you how this works and whether it might make sense for your team.

Does that sound good?"

**Purpose:** Set expectations, establish credibility, get buy-in on the agenda.

---

## Transition (30 sec)

**YOU:**
"What I want to do now is understand how your team is currently booking meetings, then I’ll show you the exact system we use to automate that work."
