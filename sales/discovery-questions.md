# Discovery Questions — AI Revenue Agent

Discovery questions and selling logic for initial conversations.

---

## Ask These Questions in Conversation

### Question 1: Response Time
**YOU:** "How fast do you currently respond to inbound leads?"

*Listen for:* Days to respond = high pain.

### Question 2: Bottleneck
**YOU:** "What's your biggest bottleneck in booking meetings right now? Is it lead qualification, follow-up, or something else?"

*Listen for:* Manual work, capacity constraints, inconsistency.

### Question 3: Personalization
**YOU:** "How personalized is your outreach today? Are you sending the same email to everyone, or customizing based on company and role?"

*Listen for:* Volume vs. quality trade-off.

### Question 4: Goals
**YOU:** "How many qualified meetings do you want to book per month?"

*Listen for:* Specific numbers = higher intent.

### Question 5: Platform Fit
**YOU:** "What systems do you currently use for CRM, email outreach, and booking meetings?"

*Listen for:* Existing tools, integration requirements, data gaps.
