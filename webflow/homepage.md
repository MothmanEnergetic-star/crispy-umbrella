## How It Works Section

### Headline
**The 7-Step Workflow**

### Subheadline
From cold lead to booked meeting in one automated system.

### Step-by-Step Breakdown

**1. Lead Enrichment**
AI automatically enriches lead data with company info, job title, LinkedIn profile, and firmographic details.

**2. Qualification**
AI scores leads against your ICP using intent and value-based criteria — only high-potential prospects move forward.

**3. Personalized Outreach**
AI generates hyper-personalized emails, LinkedIn messages, and follow-up sequences based on company data and pain points.

**4. Multi-Channel Follow-Up**
Automated sequences across email, LinkedIn, and SMS, with intelligent timing and retry logic.

**5. Objection Handling**
AI responds to common objections in real time — budget, authority, need, and timeline.

**6. Appointment Booking**
AI suggests calendar availability, sends invites, and confirms meetings automatically.

**7. CRM Automation**
All engagement data syncs automatically to HubSpot or GoHighLevel — no manual data entry.
