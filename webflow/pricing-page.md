## Pricing Tiers

### Tier 1: Startup
**$2,000 setup + $500/month**

**Best for:** Early-stage founders, small sales teams

**Includes:**
- CRM integration (HubSpot or GoHighLevel)
- Email enrichment & outreach
- 3 custom outreach templates
- Basic lead scoring
- Up to 500 leads/month
- Weekly reporting

**Typical results:**
- 50–100 additional qualified leads/month
- 10–20 booked meetings
- ~$200–500k additional pipeline

---

### Tier 2: Growth
**$5,000 setup + $1,500/month**

**Best for:** Growth-stage SaaS, agencies, revenue teams

**Includes:**
- Full integrations (CRM, email, LinkedIn, SMS, Calendly)
- Advanced lead enrichment
- Custom scoring rules
- 10 custom outreach templates
- Multi-channel follow-up automation
- Dashboard reporting
- Priority support

**Typical results:**
- 200–400 additional qualified leads/month
- 40–80 booked meetings
- ~$1M–2M additional pipeline

---

### Tier 3: Enterprise
**$10,000+ setup + $3,000–5,000/month**

**Best for:** Enterprise teams and complex workflows

**Includes:**
- Unlimited leads/month
- Custom agent training
- Unlimited templates
- Advanced analytics
- Dedicated account manager
- Custom workflows

**Typical results:**
- Scale pipeline with high-volume appointments
- Enterprise-grade execution and support
